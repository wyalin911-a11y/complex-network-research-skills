# Journal Fit Rubric

Journal fit is separate from Scientific Maturity. Use 0–10 scores.
A low score means poor fit, not poor science.

## Physical Review E (PRE)
High fit: statistical physics, phase transitions, critical behavior, analytical network processes,
universality/scaling, nontrivial collective phenomena.
Weak fit: mainly an engineering case study without a general mechanism.

## Chaos
High fit: nonlinear dynamics, bifurcation, synchronization, stability,
dynamical complex networks, emergent behavior.

## Chaos, Solitons & Fractals (CSF)
High fit: complex systems/networks, nonlinear or emergent dynamics, meaningful methodology,
and nontrivial system behavior. Do not reward complexity for its own sake.

## Reliability Engineering & System Safety (RESS)
High fit: reliability/safety meaning, cascading failure, resilience/robustness/vulnerability,
infrastructure or engineering interpretation, operational/design implications, uncertainty/risk.
Abstract models need a convincing reliability interpretation.

## Communications Physics
High fit: clear physics insight, conceptual advance, strong evidence, and relevance to
complex systems/network science beyond one narrow model. Treat it as a demanding
conceptual benchmark even when grouped with L1-family venues.

## Physical Review Letters (PRL)
High fit: one sharp conceptual discovery, simple scientific punch, general mechanism,
strong theory, broad physics relevance, little unnecessary model decoration.
A strong candidate should be expressible in one memorable scientific sentence.

## PNAS
High fit: cross-disciplinary importance, broad scientific relevance, strong validation,
and/or important real-system consequences.

## Science Advances
High fit: substantial novelty, broad interest, multi-angle evidence, and a story extending
beyond a specialized modeling exercise.

## Nature Communications
High fit: new mechanism, demonstrated generality, broad validation, complete scientific narrative,
and theory plus simulation and/or empirical evidence reinforcing one another.

## Nature / Science
Assign high fit only with exceptional evidence.
Ask whether the finding changes understanding outside network science, is supported by strong
cross-domain or empirical evidence, and is conceptually simple enough to matter broadly.

## Score interpretation
- 9–10: exceptionally strong fit if evidence succeeds
- 8–8.9: strong fit
- 7–7.9: plausible fit but important gaps remain
- 5–6.9: partial fit
- <5: poor fit

Always state the most natural current target instead of automatically selecting the
highest-impact journal.
